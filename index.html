Install

No admin rights needed.

Close Revit.
Run SRTools_Revit_Setup_1.0.9.exe — it installs the right build for each Revit version you have.
Start Revit, open the Tools tab and click Account to sign in.

Privacy

SR Tools needs a sign-in so each licence is used on one computer. To do that, it stores and sends:

  • your SR Tools account name and email,
  • your computer name and Windows user name,
  • your Revit and SR Tools version numbers.

This is used only to check your licence, deliver updates and give support. It is not sold or shared. Your Revit models and project data never leave your computer. To have your data deleted, email the support address above.
